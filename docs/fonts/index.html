Grade 50's capital A has no bar in the typeface itself — the foundry's master carries the same outline. Grades 35 and 70 both keep theirs, which is why the seven grades sit side by side here.

The three app Poppins faces are copied from app/res/font/; the rest come from tools/fetch_fonts.py.

How it is wired

Two families, seven grades, no network

  • Siteassets/site.a226215efc.css sets the site's own Poppins faces; this page declares every downloaded style from /data/fonts.json.
  • Appapp/res/font/poppins_regular.ttf, _medium and _semibold, referenced from the layouts.
  • Downloadswoff2 for the web at /assets/fonts/, woff for engines older than that, TTF for Android and desktop.
  • JSON/data/fonts.json lists each family, its faces, all three paths and their measured sizes.

A face looks like this in CSS:

@font-face { font-family: "Poppins"; src: url("/assets/fonts/poppins-400.woff2") format("woff2"); font-weight: 400; font-display: swap; }
