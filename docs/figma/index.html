Palettes

Every solid colour the site ships, then its nearest Pantone Solid Coated chip and two alternatives, each with a square of its own colour. ΔE00 is how far a chip sits from the colour: 0 is the same colour, under 3 cannot be told apart by eye, 3 to 5 is a close but visible step, and over 5 is a different shade kept only as a further option. The same table is handed over as pantone.svg.
