Landed in the box instead of the clipboard?

Every copy also lands here, so you can grab it by hand if your browser blocks the clipboard. Every glyph in both sets has been recoloured to currentColor, so an icon takes the colour of the text it sits in — drop it inside a button and it inherits the button colour.

Both sets are published as JSON, which is what the phone app reads: /data/icons.json for Lineicons and /data/coolicons.json for Coolicons.

Two free sets, both MIT: Lineicons from LineiconsHQ/Lineicons (__LINE__ glyphs) and Coolicons from krystonschwarze/coolicons (__COOL__ glyphs, line style, regular corners). Both are whole sets, not samples; the paid Pro icons sold on either vendor's site are neither included nor copied.
