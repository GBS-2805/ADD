# ADD — brand brief

**Locked 10 November 2026.** This is the identity the site and app should be built against after the engine plan. It supersedes the earlier “The Keep” working name. It does not change `PLAN.md`’s engine scope; it fixes the brand the plan will wear.

---

## 1. The brand in one line

**ADD — Art · Design · Database. Not gatekeeping creativity.**

ADD is an archive and a studio. A shelf of drawings, type and paper; a place to make something with them; a place to keep the work. No account, no upload, no subscription, no gatekeepers.

### The hierarchy

| Role | Copy |
|---|---|
| **Name** | ADD |
| **Descriptor** | Art · Design · Database |
| **Promise** | Not gatekeeping creativity. |
| **Action line** | Make it. Keep it. |
| **Campaign line** | Take up space. |
| **Manifesto line** | Art is not a subscription. |
| **Proof** | No account. No upload. No subscription. |

The descriptor is always present in the masthead or footer. “Not gatekeeping creativity” is the stance, not a replacement for the name. “Make it. Keep it.” is the action line at the Studio and Archive doors. “Take up space” and “Art is not a subscription” are campaign lines, not the masthead.

---

## 2. Positioning

**For** artists, designers, students and self-taught makers who would rather make the thing than ask permission,
**ADD is** an offline archive and studio in one place,
**that** gives them the materials (icons, type, paper, references) and the tools (type, image, shape, texture, print) to make and keep work,
**without** accounts, uploads, subscriptions or gatekeepers.

### What it is not

- Not a template farm.
- Not a stock library with a logo on it.
- Not a social feed.
- Not a museum; the archive is a working shelf.
- Not a subscription; the work stays yours.

### Brand pillars

1. **Make.** A real studio, not a preset.
2. **Keep.** An archive, not a feed.
3. **Own.** Offline, no account, no upload.
4. **Open.** Free to browse; no gatekeepers.

## 3. Architecture

```
ADD
Art · Design · Database
Not gatekeeping creativity.

Studio   — make
Archive  — keep
Editions — Wheatpaste
```

- **ADD Studio** is the editing powerhouse. In the app and product copy, “Studio” is enough; “ADD Studio” is the formal lockup.
- **The Archive** is the shelf and the database: icons, fonts, paper, references, finished plates. “Archive” is the public name. “The Keep” is retired as a brand name; it may survive as an informal word for the collection, never as a fourth identity.
- **Editions** is the print/release series. **Wheatpaste** is the name of the edition series and the 3.8 release, not the master brand. Future releases can carry their own edition names.
- The **ring** is the seal. The **wordmark** is the masthead. The **stamp** is the edition mark.

Do not add a fourth public brand without removing one.

## 4. Voice

### Principles

- **Plain, direct, unimpressed by power.** Short sentences. No art-world fog.
- **Generous, not preachy.** The work is the argument; the copy gets out of its way.
- **Archival, not nostalgic.** Newspaper grammar, not fake period costume.
- **A little hard.** “Not gatekeeping creativity” has an edge; keep it.
- **Specific.** Name the thing, the size, the edition, the file. Avoid abstraction.

### We say

Make it. Keep it. · Take up space. · Art is not a subscription. · No account. No upload. No subscription. · The work stays yours. · Open the Studio. · Enter the Archive. · Ed. 3.8 · Wheatpaste · 10.11.26 · Saved to the Archive. · Exported at 2×.

### We avoid

Unlock, empower, seamless, creative journey, content, democratize, AI-powered, next-generation, game-changing, “your all-in-one solution,” fake urgency, fake scarcity.

### Voice in one paragraph

ADD is for people who make things and want to keep them. It gives you the shelf and the studio, offline, without asking who you are. Make the work. Keep the work. No gatekeepers.

## 5. The visual law

**Clean structure, dirty ink.**

The grid, hierarchy, spacing and navigation are precise. The grunge lives in the ink, the paper and the images — never in the reading experience.

- Body copy is solid ink on clean newsprint. No texture over body text.
- Display type can be overexposed, rough, halftone or overprinted.
- Images are duotone or halftone, with blown highlights where useful.
- Controls are never distressed. A button must read as a button.
- One substrate per surface: newsprint for archive pages, rough canvas for covers and hero posters. Do not mix both textures on the same page.
- Maximum two distressed elements per view: one ink treatment and one paper/print mark.

### Surfaces

| Surface | Use | Material |
|---|---|---|
| **Archive / site** | Index, icons, fonts, tools, editions, manifesto | Newsprint cream, ink, red spot |
| **Studio** | The editor shell | Graphite, cream controls, paper artboard |
| **Covers / posters** | Hero, editions, social | Rough canvas, overexposed ink |
| **Work** | User artwork | Shown in its own colours; the chrome never recolours it |

The Studio is the press, not the newspaper. Do not skin the editor as a vintage page.

## 6. Palette

One paper, one ink, one red. No palettes, no theme switch, no acid green.

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Newsprint** | `#E9E0CB` | Archive and site surface; slightly grey cream, not sepia |
| **Ink** | `#15120E` | Body, rules, display, logo |
| **Overexposed** | `#0B0A08` | Display ink and image shadows only; never body |
| **Stamp Red** | `#DD4124` | Stamps, edition numbers, rules, large links; never body |
| **Halftone** | `#8C8476` | Captions and image tone; not body |
| **Graphite** | `#131513` | Studio shell |
| **Studio Cream** | `#F2EEE3` | Studio text and paper artboard |

### Rules

- Body text is Ink on Newsprint; check 4.5:1 or better.
- Stamp Red is a mark, not a reading colour. It is not used for body copy at small sizes.
- Overexposed is a display ink; do not use it for paragraphs.
- No gradients as brand surfaces. No glass, glow, neon or acid green.
- The red is the current Wheatpaste red; it is brand equity, not a new colour.
- The Studio uses the same red for active states, not a new accent.

## 7. Typography

All faces are already local. No new font licence is required for this system.

| Face | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Archivo Black** | Masthead, wordmark, display headlines | Uppercase, tight tracking, `-0.02em` to `-0.04em` |
| **Archivo 600/700** | Subheads, buttons, short labels | Uppercase or sentence case; never faux-bold |
| **Redaction 70** | Editorial headlines, manifesto, pull quotes | 24 px and up; texture is part of the face |
| **Redaction 35** | Letters, notes, editorial body | 16 px and up; not UI controls |
| **Plex Mono 400/600** | Datelines, edition numbers, plate captions, labels | 10–12 px, tracking `.12em`, uppercase |
| **Poppins 400/500/600** | Body and UI | 15–18 px; the friendly counterweight |

### Rules

- One display face and one body face per surface. Do not set a page in four voices.
- Redaction is for editorial voice, not for buttons or small UI.
- Plex Mono is metadata, not body copy.
- No faux italic, faux condensing or outline type in UI.
- Display type can be stacked, outlined, overprinted or halftone-filled in the Studio; the site uses one treatment at a time.

## 8. Logo

### The ring

The existing ring mark (`docs/assets/logo.svg`, `app/res/drawable/ic_mark.xml`) is retained. It is the seal: a circular, slightly imperfect mark that reads as a stamp, a plate or a press mark. Do not redraw it for this system.

- Primary lockup for print and app: ring + ADD wordmark, horizontal.
- Stacked lockup: ring above ADD, descriptor below.
- Stamp: ring alone, or ring + edition number, inside a red or ink rule.
- Favicon: ring alone at 24–32 px.
- App icon: newsprint field, ink ring, red edition mark; no gradient, no glow.

**On the newsprint site, the wordmark is the masthead.** The ring is a colophon,
favicon or app icon. It is not centred in the header, enlarged into a poster, or
repeated as a hero object. The page should read as a publication first and a logo
second.

### Wordmark

“ADD” in Archivo Black, uppercase, tight. The wordmark is a masthead, not a bespoke drawing yet. A drawn wordmark can follow once the system is applied; it must not delay the brand.

### Clear space and minimums

- Clear space: half the ring’s height on all sides.
- Minimum ring: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
- Minimum wordmark: 14 px cap height on screen.
- Never rotate, outline, stretch, emboss or place the ring on a busy photo without a paper field.
- Never use the ring as the letter O in a sentence.

### Lockup

```
[ring]  ADD
        ART · DESIGN · DATABASE
```

The descriptor sits under the wordmark in Plex Mono, tracked, at one third of the wordmark’s cap height.

## 9. Print and texture

The brand is printed, not painted.

### Materials

- **Newsprint:** visible fibre, slightly grey cream, soft show-through, no gloss.
- **Overexposed ink:** areas where the black has been pushed, with rough edges and lost highlights.
- **Halftone:** 45–65 lpi feel, visible dots, used on images and shadows.
- **Rough canvas:** heavier stock for covers and hero posters; deckle edge allowed.
- **Registration and crop marks:** hairlines at the edges, never in the reading area.
- **Stamps:** edition numbers, “KEEP”, “PROOF”, “WHEATPASTE”; rotated slightly, one per view.

### Rules

- Paper texture is procedural and tileable; no giant PNGs.
- Textures sit behind content, never over body copy.
- One stamp per view; two marks maximum (stamp + registration or plate number).
- Deckle edges are for poster/cover pieces, not every card.
- No sepia filter, faux coffee stain, fake period ad or ornamental border.
- The archive may show a user’s full-colour work on a monochrome page. Do not force the work into the house ink.

### Studio materials

The brand’s print language becomes a set of Studio materials, not a theme:

- **Newsprint** stock.
- **Wheatpaste** poster stock.
- **Overexposed** ink finish.
- **Halftone** image/ink effect.
- **Rough Canvas** cover stock.
- **Registration / crop marks / stamps** as finish layers.

This is the bridge between the brand and the engine: the archive advertises what the Studio can make.

## 10. Layout

- Broadsheet grid: 12 columns, 24 px gutter, 64 px margin at desktop; 4 columns and 20 px margin at phone.
- Hairline rules separate sections; no rounded cards, drop shadows, floating pills or glass.
- Masthead at the top of the archive: ring, ADD, descriptor, edition/date, navigation.
- Datelines, plate numbers and captions in Plex Mono.
- Work appears as plates with captions, not as a grid of SaaS cards.
- Headlines can break across lines; body measure stays 55–70 characters.
- Phone: single column, masthead compresses to ring + ADD + edition; no horizontal scroll.
- The Studio is a separate layout language: graphite shell, paper artboard, floating tool pill, peek inspector; no permanent bands.

### Front-page hierarchy

1. Masthead: ADD / Art · Design · Database.
2. Lead: **Make it. Keep it.**
3. Doors: **Open the Studio** / **Enter the Archive**.
4. Edition line: Ed. 3.8 · Wheatpaste · date.
5. Three sections: Studio / Archive / Editions.
6. Manifesto strip: Not gatekeeping creativity.
7. Work plates / archive index.
8. Footer: no account, no upload, no subscription.

## 11. Motion

GSAP is part of the identity, but it is choreography, not decoration.

- Page/masthead settle: 240 ms, `power2.out`.
- Sheet/peek: 220 ms; tool pill: 180 ms.
- Stamp press: 160 ms, one small scale step, no bounce.
- Text roll on navigation: 180 ms; no looping marquee.
- Reduced motion: all non-essential motion off; state changes still visible.
- Never animate body text while it is being read; never animate a paint stroke through GSAP.
- No parallax on the archive; the work stays still.

## 14. Governance

### Accessibility

- Body copy: 4.5:1 minimum; large display: 3:1.
- Red is not body copy at small sizes.
- Overexposed ink is display only.
- Texture must not reduce text contrast; test at 100% and 200%.
- Focus states are ink rules, not red-only.

### Performance

- Procedural/tileable textures, not large bitmaps.
- SVG filters only on display elements, never on long body runs.
- One substrate texture per page.
- The Studio’s WebGL effects must not be used to fake the brand on every surface.

### Brand checks

- No `data-palette` or theme switch on the archive.
- No acid green, four palettes or palette chips.
- No rounded SaaS cards on the archive.
- No grunge on controls or body copy.
- The ring is not redrawn, rotated or outlined.
- The ring is not a hero object on the archive; it stays at favicon, app-icon or colophon size.
- The user’s artwork is never recoloured by the chrome.
- The Studio remains graphite; the archive remains newsprint.

### Review

- Any visual change is rendered and shown with a public review link before commit or deploy.
- No production deploy until a verdict.
- The brand sheet is a study; it is not a site page and does not claim to be a screenshot of the app.
