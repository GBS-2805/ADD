ADD 3.9.1 field preview

The native engine review now carries a field as a document recipe: four or more colour spots, Gaussian reach, domain-warped flow, swirl, soften and grain. Apply it as the artboard background or as the fill of a selected text/vector layer; every stop, band, reach, dial and phase survives undo, autosave and document reopen. The field renders through the same software Skia path in preview and export, so the PNG carries the background field and field-filled type/vector, not just a look on screen. Build 3.9.1 (56), Android 13+, debuggable, arm64, not a release.
